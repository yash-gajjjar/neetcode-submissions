# 30-Day Company-Specific LeetCode Roadmap

**Goal:** Use company-tagged questions to improve interview coverage without repeating NeetCode 150 from scratch.

## Target companies

**Priority 1:** Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Apple, Bloomberg, Uber, Adobe

**Priority 2:** LinkedIn, Nvidia, Databricks, OpenAI, Atlassian, Salesforce, Coinbase

**Priority 3:** ByteDance, Flipkart, Intuit, Walmart Labs, Stripe

> The repository has uneven recent coverage. Empty 30-day / 3-month company CSVs are noted in the question-list files; they are not padded with invented questions.

## How to follow the plan

- Aim for **2–3 focused problems per day**, up to 4 when the problems are familiar.
- Solve overlap-heavy questions first. Use the 30-day file for recent tags and the 3-month file for a broader company pool.
- Mark a problem **Revision** when it is already mastered from NeetCode 150. Re-solve only when you need to test speed, edge cases or explanation.
- For a new problem, spend up to 35 minutes on Medium and 45 minutes on Hard before reviewing hints.
- For every solved problem, write down the pattern, time/space complexity and one mistake or edge case.

## Weekly checkpoints

- **Week 1:** Core overlap patterns are fluent; complete one timed mock.
- **Week 2:** Handle heaps, monotonic stacks, advanced binary search and trees; complete one timed mock.
- **Week 3:** Implement unfamiliar data structures and company-specific design problems; complete one timed mock.
- **Week 4:** Focus on speed, communication and weak areas; complete two full mocks.

## Completion targets

- 30–40 genuinely new problems, depending on difficulty.
- 20–30 focused revisions from NeetCode 150.
- At least 4 timed mocks.
- For every retained problem: explain the approach, complexity, edge cases and trade-offs without looking at notes.
